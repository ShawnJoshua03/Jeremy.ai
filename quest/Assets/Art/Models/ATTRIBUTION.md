# Model attribution

- `Resources/Patient/patient_body.bytes`: MakeHuman hm08 base mesh (`makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`,
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman), released as **CC0** in September 2020 by Data Collection AB,
  Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Converted for the desktop preview: body and eye helpers only, metres, arms
  lowered to the sides, rotated supine with the navel at the origin, stored as raw positions + triangle indices.
- Lap chole organs (liver, gallbladder, ducts, arteries) are procedural meshes (`Scripts/Exercises/LapCholeAnatomy.cs`).
- Textures in `Art/Textures/OR`: ambientCG (CC0) Fabric030 (drape normal), Tiles107 (floor).
